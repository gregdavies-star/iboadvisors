The Challenge

An IBO is the liquidity path most owners don’t know exists.

Traditional exits, including private equity and strategic sales, often require giving up your independence, accepting earn-outs, or navigating restrictive deal structures that don’t align with your vision.

There’s another path: one that provides liquidity, minimizes tax exposure, and keeps the business you built under your leadership.

That other path, that third door - that is an Independent Buyout (IBO).

The Mechanism

What is an Independent Buyout?

An Independent Buyout (IBO) allows you to buy out some or all of your ownership - effectively creating a tax-free leveraged buyout of your own company.

Instead of selling your company to a Private Equity firm - which would use company debt to buy out your shares - we execute the same type of sophisticated financial transaction but without the Private Equity.

This is the type of deal PE hopes you never learn about.

Is this you?

An IBO fits a specific kind of owner.

An Independent Buyout (IBO) works best for founders who’ve built something durable, profitable, and repeatable, and who are ready to think seriously about liquidity on their own terms. If any of the following sound familiar, it’s worth a conversation.

You run a $3M+ EBITDA profitable business

The economics of an IBO become attractive at this threshold and scale from there. You don’t need hyper-growth. You need consistency. If your financials are solid and your operations are repeatable, the structure fits.

You want liquidity without new partners or intrusive oversight

You’re not interested in selling to a strategic acquirer who’ll restructure everything or Private Equity which can be intrusive to how you run the business.

You want to take some capital off the table

Whether you want to continue to run the business or if you want to sell the business and retire - an IBO can help you achieve that goal.

You’re thinking about exit now or in 1-5 years

Maybe you’re exploring options. Maybe you’ve already started preliminary conversations with advisors or buyers. Either way, an IBO is worth modeling now - so you’re making the decision with full information, not partial options.
