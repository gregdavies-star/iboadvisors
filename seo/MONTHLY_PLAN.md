# IBO Advisors — 12-month SEO operating plan

Month 1 is September 2026. The same four-week cycle runs every month; the build, the content mix, and the authority work change month to month. Targets are ranges, not promises. Read with `STRATEGY.md` (why) and `DAILY_PROMPT.md` (the job's rules).

## Standing rules (every month)

1. **Site first, LinkedIn second.** A post goes live on iboadvisors.com, then to LinkedIn 2–3 days later, shorter, with a link to the original. Never the other way round.
2. **No IBO Advisors deal data.** Nothing about our transactions, counts, timelines, clients, or outcomes beyond what already appears on the homepage. Content is built from worked hypotheticals, public record, pattern knowledge, tools, and original survey data.
3. **Every number has a source the writer actually opened.** Public filings, court records, published data sets, or Michael. Never Reddit, X, or memory.
4. **A human reads every PR before merge.** The daily job proposes; it does not publish.
5. **Cool-downs hold.** 28 days before a title or description is touched again, 14 days for content edits, 2 posts per week maximum.

## The monthly cycle

| Week | Focus | Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Measure and plan | GSC 28d vs prior 28d by page; Ahrefs Rank Tracker for the `keywords.json` list; Reddit/X for the questions owners are asking this month | This month's 8 topics chosen (4 from Michael's LinkedIn queue, 4 from the keyword map); the month's build scoped |
| 1–4 — Publish | Two posts a week; Michael's posts expanded by the job into 1,200–2,000 words with sources, headings, internal links, IBO tie-in | 8 posts live (6 in short months) |
| 2 — Earn | Expert-quote answers, outreach batch of 10 targets, one partner touch | Links and mentions in the pipeline |
| 3 — Refresh | The job handles CTR rewrites and expansions; a human picks 2 pages for a deeper refresh | 2 pages materially improved, `Updated` date earned |
| 4 — Review | Scorecard vs targets; prune or merge pages with <10 impressions after 90 days; update `keywords.json` volumes and coverage; decide next month's build | Month-end note, next month scoped |

"Qualified conversation" = a modal submission or booked call with EBITDA ≥ $3M, source organic. Track it from Month 1 even while it reads zero.

## When to change course

- **Month 6: clicks under 300/month or referring domains under 10.** Authority is the constraint, not content. Shift Greg's hours from PR review to outreach, cut posts to 1/week, and bring forward the survey/PR push.
- **Month 9: traffic on track but zero qualified conversations.** The problem is conversion, not SEO. Work the CTAs, the modal, and landing pages for the money queries before adding pages.
- **Any month: a page loses 50%+ of clicks after an edit.** Revert it, log it in the changelog, and tighten the job's rule that caused it.
