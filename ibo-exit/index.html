The third door

Not private equity. Not a strategic. An Independent Buyout.

Door one

Sell to a strategic

A competitor pays for what they can absorb. Your brand, team, and independence usually don’t survive the integration.

Door two

Sell to private equity

They buy at today’s multiple with your company’s own borrowing capacity, hold back part of the price as an earnout, roll a slice of your equity forward at their risk, and keep most of the upside when they sell at a higher multiple.

Door three

Run the playbook yourself

An IBO finances the buyout of your own shares at a full valuation, with no sponsor on the board, no earnout, capital gains that can be deferred or eliminated, and the future upside staying with you and your team.
