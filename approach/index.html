Our Approach

A fully managed path to liquidity - without a Private Equity firm at the table.

Every Independent Buyout we run follows the same disciplined process, adapted to your business, your cap table, and your timeline. This page walks through how that process actually works, end to end.

The team behind every deal

You get one point of contact and a full deal team working behind the scenes.

IBO Advisors coordinates a network of specialists so you never have to manage four separate advisory relationships yourself.

Lending Banks

Structure and place the debt that finances the buyout, sized to your company’s cash flow and risk profile.

Transaction Lawyers

Draft and negotiate the purchase agreement, governance terms, and closing documents.

ESOP Firms

Handle trustee representation, valuation, and compliance where an ESOP is part of the structure.

Tax Advisors

Model and confirm the tax treatment before you sign anything, so there are no surprises at close.

How it works

Five steps, fully managed - here’s what happens at each one.

01

Initial Evaluation

We assess your business, ownership structure, and liquidity goals in a confidential conversation. You’ll share financials and growth plans, and we’ll tell you plainly whether an IBO fits - no cost or commitment either way.

02

Structuring

We design a transaction structure specific to your company: how much of your ownership you sell, how the deal is taxed, what governance terms you keep, and how future upside is shared.

03

Financing

We bring the structure to our lending network and secure financing sized to your business’s cash flow - so the debt that funds your liquidity doesn’t put unreasonable strain on operations.

04

Execution

Legal, tax, and financial advisors finalize documentation and close the transaction. We manage the process so it stays confidential and disruption to daily operations stays minimal.

05

Ongoing Advisory

After close, we stay involved - helping you manage the new structure, plan for any follow-on liquidity, and navigate decisions as the business continues to grow.

Common questions

About the process.

How long does the process take?

It depends on your company’s complexity and how quickly financing comes together, but the path is the same five steps for every deal - from the first confidential conversation through close and beyond.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Every conversation starts under confidentiality, and nothing about your business, financials, or intent to explore a transaction is shared outside the deal team without your approval.

What if an IBO turns out not to fit?

We tell you early. The Initial Evaluation step exists specifically to confirm fit before either side invests real time in structuring or financing.

Do I have to give up day-to-day control?

No. The structure is built around existing leadership staying in place. You’re financing a buyout of ownership, not bringing in a new operating partner.
